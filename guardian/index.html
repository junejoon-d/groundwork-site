What Groundwork is

Groundwork helps people find others nearby to train with: runs, gym sessions, rides. Those sessions happen in person, which is why we don't let anyone under 18 use it without a parent or guardian agreeing first.

What you would be agreeing to

  • You'll be asked to confirm your identity, and then shown exactly what you're agreeing to before anything is approved.
  • You'll be able to see who they connect with, read any conversation between them and an adult, and pause their account at any time.

Approve or decline

Approval is completed in the Groundwork app on your phone.
