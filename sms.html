Groundwork text messages

Groundwork sends text messages only for two purposes: sign-in verification codes, and asking a parent or guardian to approve a minor's account. We send no marketing texts.

How a parent or guardian is asked

  1. A person under 18 signs up in the Groundwork app. The app tells them it needs a parent or guardian's approval and asks for that adult's name, email address and phone number.
  2. The guardian receives one text from Groundwork with a single-use link to review what the app does and approve or decline.
  3. Every text identifies Groundwork and says: Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

How a member signs in by text

When a member enters their phone number to sign in, we text a verification code. Message and data rates may apply.
