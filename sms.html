Groundwork text messages

Groundwork sends text messages only for two purposes: sign-in verification codes, and updates for a parent or guardian who has chosen to receive texts about a minor's account. We send no marketing texts.

How a parent or guardian is asked

  1. A person under 18 signs up in the Groundwork app. The app tells them it needs a parent or guardian's approval and asks for that adult's name and email address only. We do not ask for, and never text, a phone number the young person types in.
  2. The guardian receives an email with a single-use link to a page on this site, which explains what Groundwork does and what they would be agreeing to.
  3. On that page the guardian may tick an unchecked box and enter their own mobile number to receive text updates. The box reads: "Yes, text me about this account. I agree to receive text messages from Groundwork by Danny Chong at the number below: a confirmation, reminders to approve, and account notices." Agreeing is not required to approve the account.
  4. We then send one confirmation text. Every text identifies Groundwork by Danny Chong and says: Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

How a member signs in by text

When a member enters their phone number to sign in, we text a verification code. Message and data rates may apply.
