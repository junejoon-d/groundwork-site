Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026. This policy explains what Danny Chong ("Groundwork", "we") collects through the Groundwork app and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address and/or phone number, and your display name.
  • Age information: we record whether you are an adult or a minor. We do not store your date of birth.
  • Profile and matching details: the activities you do, your availability, the neighborhood you train in, and what you choose to add to your profile.
  • Location: a neighborhood you choose and, only if you turn it on, a coarse live location. Exact coordinates are not shown to other people.
  • Messages between you and the training partners you have matched with.
  • Parent or guardian details for accounts under 18: a minor provides a parent or guardian's name, email address and phone number so we can ask them for consent. We use that contact information only for the consent process and for notices about the minor's account.
  • Identity verification: where we ask for it, an identity check is performed by our verification provider; we receive the result, not your ID images.

How we use it

To run the app: show you compatible people, let matched partners message each other, keep accounts safe, obtain parent or guardian consent for minors, send you the messages you request (such as sign-in codes), and respond to reports of misuse.

Text messages (SMS)

We send text messages for sign-in verification and to ask a parent or guardian to approve a minor's account. We do not send marketing text messages. Phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are used only for these purposes.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers to operate the app, and they may process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in), Stream (messaging), Twilio and SendGrid (text and email delivery), Stripe (identity verification) and, if you connect a wearable, Spike. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

Safety and legal

We may disclose information where required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, delete a match, and ask us to delete your account and data by emailing groundwork.app.help@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.

Children

People under 18 can use Groundwork only after a parent or guardian consents. A parent or guardian can review and withdraw consent at any time.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Contact: groundwork.app.help@gmail.com.
