Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026. This policy explains what Danny Chong ("Groundwork", "we") collects through the Groundwork app and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address and/or phone number, and your display name.
  • Age information: we record whether you are an adult or a minor. We do not store your date of birth.
  • Profile and matching details: the activities you do, your availability, the neighborhood you train in, and what you choose to add to your profile.
  • Location: a neighborhood you choose and, only if you turn it on, a coarse live location. Exact coordinates are not shown to other people.
  • Messages between you and the training partners you have matched with.
  • Parent or guardian details for accounts under 18: a minor provides a parent or guardian's name and email address so we can ask them for consent. We use that contact information only for the consent process and for notices about the minor's account. We do not ask a minor for a parent or guardian's phone number. A parent or guardian may choose to give us their own mobile number to receive text updates.
  • Identity verification: where we ask for it, an identity check is performed by our verification provider; we receive the result, not your ID images.

How we use it

To run the app: show you compatible people, let matched partners message each other, keep accounts safe, obtain parent or guardian consent for minors, send you the messages you request, and respond to reports of misuse.

Text messages (SMS)

We send a text message only to a parent or guardian who chooses to receive one, to confirm they are signed up for updates about a minor's account. We never text a number just because someone else supplied it. We do not send marketing text messages. Phone numbers and SMS opt-in data are used only for these purposes.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who processes data for us

We use service providers to operate the app, and they may process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in), Stream (messaging), Twilio and SendGrid (text and email delivery), Stripe (identity verification) and, if you connect a wearable, Spike. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

Safety and legal

We may disclose information where required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, delete a match, and delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account). Your account is hidden straight away and erased for good after 30 days; sign back in during those 30 days to keep it. Erasing removes your profile, matches, plans, training logs, photos and ID verification, and we ask our verification provider to delete the ID photos, selfie and date of birth it holds. Messages you sent stay in the other person's conversation, shown as "Deleted account". If you filed, or were the subject of, a safety report, we keep the report without your name for up to a year after it is resolved. If you cannot use the app, email groundwork.app.help@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.

Children

People under 18 can use Groundwork only after a parent or guardian consents. A parent or guardian can review and withdraw consent at any time.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Contact: groundwork.app.help@gmail.com.
